Orbit-Transfer · live demo

Hybrid P2P / edge-relay file transfer with rateless fountain codes (LDPC + HDPC). Rust codec compiled to WASM, running in your browser. Open a demo:

WebRTC browser-to-browser transfer

Drag & drop a file, share a ?room= link, and transfer P2P over a WebRTC data channel — with a simulated-loss slider to show the rateless code absorbing packet loss.

Fountain codec in the browser

Encode + decode any file entirely in the tab, and watch the decode overhead stay near 1× even at high simulated loss.
